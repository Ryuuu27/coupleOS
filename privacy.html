Privacy Policy

Couple OS · Last updated: October 2026

Couple OS (couplsos.vercel.app) is a shared dashboard for two people: a calendar, budget, groceries, chores, diary, photo album and goals. This page explains what information the app keeps and how it is used.

What we store

  • Your email address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see your password in plain text.
  • The content you add: names, calendar events, expenses, grocery items, chores, diary entries, photos, captions and goals. It is saved in Google Firebase (Firestore) under your account so the two of you see the same dashboard.
  • Small settings saved in your browser on your own device, such as notification choices.

Google Calendar

If you press “Sync Google Calendar”, the app asks for permission to create, update and delete the calendar events that Couple OS itself adds to your Google Calendar. It uses this only to copy your Couple OS events into your calendar and keep them up to date. The access token stays in your browser's memory and is not stored on our servers. We do not use your Google Calendar data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone.

Couple OS's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your information

Only people who log in with your account. We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with third parties. The app runs on Google Firebase and Vercel, which process data on our behalf to host the service.

Calendar link (optional)

If you choose to use the subscribed calendar link, a calendar file containing your event names and dates is published at a private web address. Anyone who has that address can read it, so keep it to yourself.

Deleting your data

You can delete events, expenses, entries and photos inside the app at any time. To delete your whole account and its data, or to ask any question about this policy, email jersonnalla092@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
